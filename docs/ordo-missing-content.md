## Content needed per coverage type

- **common-plus-proper** (e.g. `mary-mother-church`): Hours — Common (blessed-virgin-mary) for psalmody and most texts; proper collect, hymns/antiphons where the diocesan proper has them. Readings — Proper readings or the Common of the Lectionary (required: the weekday readings are not allowed). Route — New `days` document (Common completed at import, like patchs/generate-db-feasts.mjs).
- **common-plus-collect** (e.g. `our-lady-help-of-christians`): Hours — Common (blessed-virgin-mary) + proper collect (and a hagiographic second reading of the Office of Readings if available). Readings — Optional: the weekday readings are the default (GIRM 357); saint/Common readings as an alternative. Route — New `days` document built from the Common (generate-db-feasts) with the proper collect.
- **proper** (e.g. `holy-name-of-jesus`): Hours — Proper collect (and proper antiphons/hymns where the Polish/Latin LH has them); the rest from the Christmas weekday. Readings — Proper readings (Missal/Lectionary). Route — New `days` document with proper texts.
- **common** (e.g. `saturday-blessed-virgin-mary`): Hours — Common of the BVM (texts of the Saturday memorial are the Common; no proper texts exist). Readings — Weekday readings or the Common of the BVM (Lectionary, Commons). Route — A template route or a `days` document built from common-texts/blessed-virgin-mary.

Common templates live in `patchs/feasts/common-texts/`; `patchs/generate-db-feasts.mjs` completes a
proper source file with its Common. Readings on memorials are optional (weekday readings are the default).
